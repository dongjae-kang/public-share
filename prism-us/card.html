Flooding in Missouri Prompts State of Emergency and at Least 100 Rescues

Flash flooding swept through Missouri on Friday, July 11th, 2026, following torrential rainfall that brought 6 to 12 inches of water to the region in a short period. The governor declared a state of emergency in response to the widespread flooding across central and southeastern Missouri. Over 200 people, mostly children and staff, became stranded at Camp Taum Sauk in Lesterville when floodwaters cut off all roads to the campground. Eight Black Hawk helicopters conducted rescues at the campground and in surrounding areas, with emergency crews performing at least 300 rescues statewide. A 23-year-old woman was reported missing and presumed swept away in Crawford County. Palm Beach airport officially renamed President Donald J. Trump International Airport

South Florida's Palm Beach airport renamed President Donald J. Trump International

Palm Beach International Airport was officially renamed President Donald J. Trump International Airport on July 9 after Florida lawmakers passed a bill earlier in the year. The renaming makes Trump the first sitting U.S. president to have an airport named after him. Eric Trump celebrated the occasion by landing what he called "Trump Force One" as the first plane at the renamed airport. The airport is located near Trump's Mar-a-Lago estate in Palm Beach, and the rebranding included new signage throughout the terminal. According to Al Jazeera English, the move prompted mixed reactions, with supporters welcoming the tribute while critics argued it was in poor taste. Coverage of the event split notably between outlets presenting it as a significant honor and those focusing on negative public reaction.

02The StoryEnough to understand it from this card alone

The renaming followed legislative action by Florida lawmakers to change the airport's name from Palm Beach International Airport. The move represented a high-profile tribute to Trump's political support and his deep ties to Florida, where he maintains his Mar-a-Lago resort. Eric Trump participated in the opening celebration, conducting a tour of the airport and piloting the inaugural flight to land there. The renaming stands as an unusual honor for a sitting president, distinguishing it from typical airport naming conventions. The event generated divided public response, with some viewing it as recognition of Trump's prominence and others objecting to the decision on various grounds.
